Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2025

Kemutan is committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

We use analytics to collect anonymized, non-identifying information to improve the app. This may include:

  • Anonymous usage events (screen views, button taps)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics
  • Non-personal device identifiers

Device permissions

The app requests camera access to scan printed photos and access to your photo library to display or save content you choose. These permissions are only used for app functionality and are not shared without your consent.

How we use data

Collected analytics help us improve features and stability. We do not sell personal data. Any analytics we use are configured to avoid collecting personal content.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted services (for example, Firebase) for analytics and crash reporting. Please review their privacy pages for details: Firebase Privacy.
