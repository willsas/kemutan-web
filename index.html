Main Features

  • iCloud sync: Photos and videos are stored in iCloud and automatically sync across devices signed into the same iCloud account.
  • Shared Albums (CloudKit): Create Shared Albums to build Memories with family and friends; invite collaborators and contribute photos and videos to shared collections.
  • AR Playback: Scan a printed photo and the linked video plays over the photo in augmented reality.
  • Privacy & control: Owners control sharing and membership of shared albums; data uses Apple Cloud services.
