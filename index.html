what's inside —

Four things it does that nothing else does

Stories that stay shut

The reel is locked until the dump holds enough memories. Everyone in it sees the same countdown, so it's a reason to keep dumping.

Printed photos that move

Point the camera at a real, printed photo. The video mounts itself in a paper print right on top of it, in AR, with the sound.

Dump together

Built on Apple's shared albums, so a dump belongs to everyone in it. No accounts to chase, no upload limits to argue with.

Yours, in your iCloud

Photos and videos live in your own iCloud and sync across your devices. We don't keep a copy, and there's nothing to sell.
